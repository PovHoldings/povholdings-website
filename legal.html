Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026 · Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA)

1. Responsible party and information officer

The responsible party for your personal information is Point of View Holdings (Pty) Ltd, trading as POV Holdings. Our information officer is Robert Christopher Denny, reachable at info@povholdings.co.za.

2. What we collect

  • Enquiries and quotes: your name, mobile number, dealership or business name, the service you're interested in and anything you write to us.
  • Purchases: your name, email, phone, delivery address and order details. Card payments are handled by our payment provider; we do not receive your card number.
  • Email updates: your email address, if you sign up for POV alerts and updates.
  • Reviews: the name, business, rating and text you choose to post, linked to your signed-in account so you can edit your own review.
  • Website usage: if you accept analytics cookies, pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location, and how you arrived (for example the advert or link you clicked).
  • Client work: photos and video of vehicles and dealership premises. If people appear in our content, we ask for their permission.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to enquiries, prepare quotes and deliver the services you buy.
  • To process orders, payments and deliveries.
  • To show reviews and improve our website and services.
  • To send you POV updates, alerts and offers by email only if you signed up for them or are an existing client, as section 69 of POPIA requires. Every email has an unsubscribe option, or email info@povholdings.co.za to be removed.
  • To meet our legal and tax obligations.

We rely on your consent, the need to perform a contract with you, our legitimate interest in running and improving our business, and compliance with the law, as POPIA allows.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate, under confidentiality and data-protection obligations: our hosting and website platform, our online store and payment provider, WhatsApp (Meta) when you message us, Google Analytics if you accept analytics cookies, and our partners MiLife (POV Performance) and ConversionCo (POV Meta) where you buy their joint service. Some of these providers store data outside South Africa; we use providers that offer adequate protection as required by section 72 of POPIA.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for up to 2 years, client and transaction records for 5 years as tax law requires, and reviews until you delete them or ask us to. After that we delete or anonymise it.

6. How we protect it

We use access controls, secure providers and encrypted connections to protect your information. If we become aware of a breach that affects you, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

7. Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to us using it, and withdraw consent or opt out of marketing at any time. Email info@povholdings.co.za. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Information Regulator: complaints.IR@inforegulator.org.za, www.inforegulator.org.za.

Refunds and cancellations

Last updated 30 October 2026 · ECTA sections 42 to 44 and the CPA

Online purchases: 7-day cooling-off

For goods bought online, such as POV Collection clothing, you may cancel without reason within 7 days of receiving the goods, under section 44 of ECTA. Return the item unused and in its original condition; the only cost to you is the direct cost of returning it. We refund the full purchase price within 30 days of cancellation.

POV Package (digital course)

The POV Package gives you immediate access to course material. When you buy it, you ask us to start the service straight away and agree that, once access has begun, the 7-day cooling-off right no longer applies, as section 42(2) of ECTA allows for services that begin with your consent. If you have not yet accessed the course, contact us within 7 days of purchase for a full refund.

Defective goods

Under the CPA you may return defective goods within 6 months for a repair, replacement or refund. Contact us first so we can sort it out quickly.

Services (photography, video, coaching, ads)

Monthly services may be cancelled with 30 days' written notice unless your service agreement says otherwise. Work already completed and costs already incurred are payable. Shoots cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice may be charged.
