Built on people

Great dealerships aren't built on stock and systems alone.

Dealerships invest heavily in leads, stock and sales processes. That investment only pays off when the people behind it are equipped, developed and supported. POV Performance bridges that gap.

Our approach

Understand. Develop. Measure.

Good to know

Is this a once-off training day?

No. Training is followed by ongoing development on MiLife, with weekly reporting for management.

Does it take salespeople off the floor?

As little as possible. Sessions are planned around your trading hours, and MiLife runs on a phone.

What do managers see?

Weekly insights into each salesperson's activity, development and progress.

With an introductory meeting at your dealership. We'll show you the approach and MiLife, then quote for your team.
