The partnership

POV brings the floor. MiLife brings the platform.

MiLife is a South African learning and opportunity platform, built "for the people, by the people". Short, practical courses with certificates recognised by employers, progress tracking and career support, all on the phone. POV coaches your team in person; MiLife keeps the learning going between visits.

How it works

Floor visitWe meet your sales team and managers and see how the floor runs.
Style profileEach salesperson takes MiLife's free 3-minute personality profile.
Coach & learnOn-floor coaching from POV, with MiLife courses in between.
Track & certifyProgress is tracked and completed courses earn certificates.

Need salespeople?

Through the MiLife Bridge, dealerships can load a job spec and MiLife shortlists and refers candidates from its learner community.

  • Register your dealership on the MiLife portal
  • Load your job specification: role, skills, experience, location
  • Approved roles go live to MiLife learners
  • Learners apply with their CVs
  • MiLife shortlists, matches and refers candidates to you
  • Every candidate has trained on the platform

Good to know

Who is it for?

Dealership sales teams of any size, from new salespeople to sales managers. Hatfield VW Pretoria was our first floor, with a team of 35.

Does it take salespeople off the floor?

As little as possible. Coaching happens on site around your trading hours, and the MiLife courses are short and run on a phone.

Do salespeople get certificates?

Yes. Completed MiLife courses come with certificates recognised by employers.
